## 🎯 Problem Statement

Major ports can experience severe congestion when large numbers of vessels arrive simultaneously, creating delays across global supply chains. Port operators often rely on manual spreadsheets and reactive decision-making to allocate berths, cranes, and yard space, making it difficult to identify congestion before it becomes critical.

Our project addresses this problem by providing port operators with an intelligent system that predicts congestion hotspots in advance and recommends optimised operational decisions.

## 💡 Solution

We are building an AI-powered Container Congestion Predictor & Port Operations Optimiser that analyses vessel schedules, berth capacity, crane availability, and port conditions to predict where congestion is likely to occur.

The system provides early congestion alerts, alternate routing recommendations, optimised berth and crane assignments, and a continuously updated 72-hour operational plan. This allows shift supervisors and port operators to move from reactive decision-making to proactive planning.

How it works

Input Data → AI Analysis → Congestion Prediction → Optimisation → Actionable Plan

1.Vessel schedules and port capacity data are provided to the system.
2.The system analyses expected vessel arrivals and available port resources.
3.Potential congestion hotspots are identified before queues become critical.
4.Alternative routing strategies are recommended when required.
5.Berths and cranes are optimised based on expected demand.
6.A 72-hour operational plan is generated for port supervisors.

## ✨ Key Features

- **Feature 1:**Congestion Prediction — Predicts potential port congestion hotspots using vessel schedules, berth capacity, and operational data.
- **Feature 2:** Alternate Route Recommendations — Suggests alternative routing strategies when congestion is predicted.
- **Feature 3:** Smart Berth Allocation — Recommends efficient berth assignments based on vessel arrivals and available capacity.
- **Feature 4:** Crane Assignment Optimisation — Helps distribute available cranes efficiently to reduce vessel waiting and handling delays.
- **Feature 5:** 72-Hour Operations Plan — Generates an actionable plan for port supervisors covering the next 72 hours.

## ⚠️ Known Limitations

> Be honest — judges appreciate transparency over overclaiming.

- Limitation 1: Prediction depends on data quality — The accuracy of congestion predictions depends on the availability and quality of vessel schedule and port-capacity data.
- Limitation 2: Simulated/limited real-world data — The prototype may use historical, simulated, or limited datasets rather than live operational data from an actual port.
- Limitation 3: Prototype optimisation — The current optimisation recommendations are designed as a decision-support system and may require validation by experienced port operators before production deployment.


## 🏅 What We're Most Proud Of

We are most proud of transforming a traditionally reactive port-management problem into a proactive, AI-driven decision-support system.

Instead of waiting for vessels to form long queues, our solution aims to identify congestion before it becomes critical and provide port supervisors with practical recommendations for routing, berth allocation, crane assignment, and resource planning.

The most important aspect of our project is the 72-hour operational plan, which converts complex port data and predictions into clear, actionable decisions that can help improve port efficiency and reduce vessel waiting time.
